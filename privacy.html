Privacy

Covers sotakece.com and sere.sotakece.com. Last updated 9 November 2026.

Sota Kece is a small social space made for Fijians and friends everywhere. Sere is the part of it that makes a photo sing. Both are run by Dimau Group. This page says, in plain words, what we keep about you and why.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address, or the name, email and profile picture your Google or Facebook account gives us when you sign in with it. We never see your Google or Facebook password.
  • What you put in. Your profile, the look of your space, your posts, comments, gifts, your friends list, the songs you add, and chats and calls you take part in.
  • Sere. The photo and the sound (or the words you type) that you use to make a sere, and the finished video. The photo and sound are deleted from our side once the video is made. The video stays until you remove it.
  • Plain technical records. The kind any website keeps so it works and stays safe — your device type, rough location from your internet address, and error logs. We do not run advertising trackers.

Why we use it

To run the service you signed up for: show your space to you and your friends, play your music, post your seres to the Talanoa Wall, and keep the place safe. Nothing else. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is kept and who helps us

  • Supabase stores the account and the content, in a data centre in Sydney, Australia.
  • Vercel serves the website and runs the small bits of code behind it.
  • Replicate turns your photo and sound into the sere video. It gets only that photo and that sound, for that job.
  • Google and Facebook are only involved if you choose to sign in with them.

Who can see your content

Your space and your posts are visible to the people you connect with on Sota Kece. Finished seres go to the Talanoa Wall, which members can see. Chats and calls are between the people in them.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. Delete something and it goes. Delete your Sota Kece profile and everything in Sota Kece and Sere goes with it — see Delete my data.

Your rights

You can see, change and delete what we hold about you from inside the app, or by deleting your account. If you want a copy of your data or have a question, email support@fijiinvestment.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Children

Sota Kece is for people aged 13 and over. If you think a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we will say so in the app.
