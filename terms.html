These are the rules for using Sota Kece and Sere. They are short on purpose.

Your account

Your content

  • What you post stays yours. You give us permission to store it and show it to the people you share it with, so the service can work. That permission ends when you delete it.
  • Only post photos, sounds and words you have the right to use. For Sere, only use photos of yourself or of people who have said yes.
  • No bullying, hate, threats, nudity, or anything illegal. No spam. We can remove content or close accounts that break this, without warning if it is serious.

Sere

  • Sere makes a video of a photo moving to a sound. It is for fun and for sharing with your people. Do not use it to trick people into thinking someone said or sang something they did not.

The service

Ending things
