# Coins, tipping and payouts (10 November 2026)

The blueprint's Day 1 feature 4. Coins are a closed loop on top of the FJD wallet that already
exists in Sota Kece: a member buys a coin pack from their wallet, tips coins on Talanoa Wall posts,
and the author moves the coins they earned back into their own wallet. Sota Kece keeps a fee on
every tip. Everything is built and tested; nothing moves until the owner flips two switches.

## The hold

The wallet is held until the Reserve Bank of Fiji approves it (`window.SOTA_WALLET_ON = false`,
tracker launch order of 31 October). Coins can only come from the wallet, so they sit under the same
hold, twice over:

- in the page, every coin surface (the tip button on wall cards, the coin cards in the wallet) is
  drawn only while `SOTA_WALLET_ON` is true;
- in the database, `coin_settings.buy_on` and `coin_settings.payout_on` are `false`, so even a
  direct call cannot buy a pack or pay coins out. `tips_on` is true but no one holds coins.

Turning it on, after approval: `update bulavata.coin_settings set buy_on = true, payout_on = true;`
and `SOTA_WALLET_ON = true` in `index.html`. Nothing else changes.

## Money model

- 1 coin = FJ$0.10 (`coin_sene`). Packs: bilo 50 for FJ$5, tanoa 100 + 10 free for FJ$10, lovo
  250 + 50 for FJ$25, vanua 500 + 150 for FJ$50 (`coin_bundles`, editable).
- A tip of N coins: the giver's balance drops by N; Sota Kece keeps `floor(N × fee_bps / 10000)`
  (30% by default, `fee_bps`); the author's **earned** pile grows by the rest. Earned coins are a
  separate pile from spendable coins: a tip received cannot be re-tipped, only paid out, which keeps
  circular tipping from laundering fees.
- Payout: earned coins become wallet money at `coin_sene` per coin, from `payout_min_coins` (100).
  The money then moves like any wallet money (the wallet's own daily limit and status apply).
- Limits: `max_tip` 500 per tip, `daily_tip_cap` 500 per member per day.

## Data layer (`supabase/migrations/20261110200000_sota_coins.sql`, schema `bulavata`)

- `coin_settings` (one row: rate, fee, the three switches, limits, running `platform_fees`).
- `coin_bundles`, `coin_accounts` (`balance`, `earned`, lifetime counters), `coin_ledger`
  (append-only, signed `delta`, `group_id` ties the legs of one movement; `owner` null is Sota Kece's
  fee), `coin_tips` (one row per tip, with the note).
- `talanoa_posts` gains `tip_count` and `tip_coins`; `bv_talanoa_feed` returns both.
- The wallet ledger (`transactions.kind`) gains `coin_buy` and `coin_payout`; Activity gains `tip`.
- RLS: a member reads their own account, ledger and the tips they gave or received; settings and
  packs are readable; nothing is writable. Every movement goes through a function.
- Functions (members): `bv_coins()` (balance, earned, switches, packs, last 20 ledger lines),
  `bv_coin_tip(post, coins, note)`, `bv_coins_buy(bundle, idem)`, `bv_coins_payout(coins)`,
  `bv_coin_tips_for(post)`. Errors are words the page translates: `NOT_ENOUGH_COINS`,
  `SELF_TIP`, `DAILY_CAP`, `COINS_BUY_OFF`, `PAYOUT_OFF`, `BELOW_MINIMUM`, `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`,
  `WALLET_DORMANT` and so on.
- Concurrency: a per-member advisory lock serialises a member's own movements; a tip locks both
  accounts in one statement in id order, so two members tipping each other cannot deadlock;
  purchases are idempotent on the wallet reference.

## Page

- `sota-coins.js` / `sota-coins.css`: a 🪙 button on every wall card that is not yours (lit once the
  post has coins; the author sees the total as a label); an inline tip sheet under the post with
  presets 5 / 10 / 25 / 50, another amount, a 60-character note; the coin cards in the wallet view
  (coins, packs, earned pile with "Move to my wallet", recent ledger lines). Errors read as words.
- `sota-pulse.js` draws the button and hands the tap to `SotaCoins`; `sota-plus.js` words the
  Activity line ("@bo 🪙 12 coins, tipped your post “vinaka”"); `index.html` starts the module at
  boot and places the cards in `viewWallet`. Release v171.

## Verified

- `node tests/run-coins-db.cjs` (isolated PGlite, in the Talanoa privacy workflow): 65 SQL checks +
  preservation snapshot. Anon denied; switches off by default and refused as words; a purchase moves
  FJ$10.00 once and is idempotent; a dormant wallet cannot buy; a tip splits 7 / 3, writes three
  legs under one group that sum to zero, counts on the post and tells the author; self-tip, missing
  post, zero, over-maximum, over-balance, daily cap and tips-off all refused; a payout of 10 coins
  becomes FJ$1.00, wakes a dormant wallet and ties the wallet leg to the coin leg by reference; every
  balance, every earned pile and the fee total equal their ledger, and coins minted minus paid out
  equal coins held plus fees; a removed post keeps every balance.
- `tests/coins.test.cjs` (7): sheet markup and words, the button only while the wallet is on, the
  full tip flow, a refused tip, the wallet cards on and off, the Activity line, the page wiring.
- Full suite green with and without jsdom. The tip sheet and the wallet cards rendered at 390px.

## Not verified here

- A purchase against the live wallet (the engine's top-up and the wallet itself are held).
- Activity rendering of a real tip on the hosted app.
